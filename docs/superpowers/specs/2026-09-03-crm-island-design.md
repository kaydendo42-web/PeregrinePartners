# CRM takes the seventh slot, rostering falls under admin

Kayden's call, 2026-09-03. Two changes, one shape: the roster stops being a
department of its own and becomes work Admin does, and the guest relationship
becomes the department that slot leaves free.

## What moves

**The Floor (`/platform`).** Still six islands, so the page's "six departments"
and its facts are untouched.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| 007 Rostering, its own island | gone; Deputy, the draft and award rates are desks on Admin |
| 004 Admin, three desks | five desks: the phone, the inbox, the website, the roster, timesheets |
| 003 Marketing carried a Guest CRM desk | Marketing keeps Instagram, Meta Ads and Google only |
| — | 007 CRM, ours, on the island Rostering vacated |

CRM is the second island tagged **ours**, so it wears the same blush tint as
Bookings. That tint means "we built this", not "this department is special", so
two of them is the rule working rather than a collision.

The island runs six desks: the guest record, follow-ups, loyalty, email,
messages and win-backs. Loyalty is the tracker on it: cards counting up to the
tenth visit, and regulars who have gone quiet. Marketing's two guest tasks (the
thank-you notes and the sixty-day win-back) move here, where they belong.

No new **needs you** task. The modelled morning leaves exactly two decisions on
the desk, the tomato order and the function quote, and the headline, the facts
and the empty state all count on that.

## The rack, on the home page

Nine departments, still nine. 007 changes hands and 008 absorbs the roster.

- **007 CRM & Loyalty**, tagged Ours, runs on Peregrine, waits on anything that
  reaches a guest.
- **008 Rostering & Admin**, the roster drafted against forecast and award
  rates, timesheets, renewals. Takes the shift-stack drawing that was 007's,
  which is the picture of its leading claim now; the certificate drawing it had
  is deleted rather than kept unused.
- **009 The Till** stays exactly as it is. It is the one department that only
  reads, and that claim earns its own tab.

New art for 007: the guest card, a loyalty strip on its tenth punch, and a
follow-up leaving. Same isometric line language as the rest of the rack.
