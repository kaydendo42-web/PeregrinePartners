# Peregrine Office CRM

The private founder and client workspace is named **Peregrine Office**. Its shared
display name lives in `lib/brand.ts`. The public website branding is outside this
change.

The name applies to the founder sidebar, workspace picker and headers; the client
console; private page titles; and new authenticator enrolments. Existing
authenticator factors retain their labels and credentials.

The internal founder workspace is renamed in place. Its ID, `peregrine` slug,
client links, access policies and saved records are retained. Existing website
domains, email addresses, repository identity and Vercel project identity remain
usable. No replacement account or domain has been provisioned.
